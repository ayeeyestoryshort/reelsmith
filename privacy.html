ReelSmith Privacy Policy

Effective Nov 6, 2026

ReelSmith keeps your data on your own computer. It has no servers or user accounts of its own, collects no analytics, and never sells or shares your data with advertisers.

1. About this policy

This policy explains what information ReelSmith, a desktop application for creating and posting short videos, handles when you use it, including when you connect your TikTok, YouTube or Facebook account. It applies to the ReelSmith app and its optional phone companion. Each platform and service you connect has its own privacy policy, which applies to the data they hold.

ReelSmith uses YouTube API Services to upload videos to your YouTube channel. When you connect YouTube, Google’s handling of your information is described in the Google Privacy Policy, and your use of YouTube is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service.

2. Information ReelSmith collects

From the platforms you connect, ReelSmith requests only the permissions it needs to post for you:

  • TikTok: basic profile (user.info.basic: your TikTok user ID, display name and profile picture) and posting (video.publish, video.upload). With these, ReelSmith also receives your account’s posting options (such as allowed privacy settings and maximum video length) and the status of each post.
  • YouTube (Google): uploading videos to your channel (youtube.upload) and reading your channel’s name and the processing status of the videos you upload (youtube.readonly).
  • Facebook (Meta): seeing the Facebook Pages you manage and publishing videos and Reels to the Page you choose (pages_show_list, pages_read_engagement, pages_manage_posts). ReelSmith receives the Page’s name and ID and the status of each post.
  • Access tokens that each platform issues so ReelSmith can act for you. ReelSmith never sees your passwords.

That you create or enter in ReelSmith:

  • Topics, scripts, captions, hashtags, narration audio, images and finished videos.
  • Voice recordings, if you record narration yourself.
  • Settings, and the API keys you enter for the services ReelSmith uses.

ReelSmith does not collect your location, contacts, browsing history, or usage analytics, and it does not use cookies or trackers.

3. How it’s used and where it goes

ReelSmith uses your information only to make the videos you ask for and post them where you choose. It is stored on your computer: access tokens and API keys in your operating system’s secure credential store (such as Windows Credential Manager), and everything else in ReelSmith’s folder on your computer.

Data leaves your computer only when a feature you use needs a service:

Service What it receives When
TikTok Your video, caption and posting settings When you connect TikTok or press Post
YouTube (Google) Your video, title and description When you connect YouTube or post to it
Facebook (Meta) Your video and caption When you post to your Facebook Page
Anthropic Your video topic or story idea When ReelSmith writes a script
ElevenLabs The narration text When ReelSmith records narration
fal.ai or OpenAI Image descriptions and character portraits When ReelSmith makes images
Openverse and Wikimedia Commons Your image search words When you search for free stock images

ReelSmith’s optional phone companion connects only to your own computer over your home network, using an encrypted connection.

4. Sharing

ReelSmith does not sell, rent or trade your information, and does not share it for advertising. Beyond the services in section 3, information is disclosed only if required by law.

ReelSmith’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Information received from TikTok and Meta is used only as their developer terms allow.

5. Keeping and deleting your data

  • Platform data (account details and tokens) is kept only while that platform is connected. Disconnecting a platform in ReelSmith’s settings deletes its tokens and account details from your computer.
  • Removing ReelSmith’s access on each platform, in addition to disconnecting in ReelSmith:
    • TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security and permissions → Apps and services permissions, then remove ReelSmith.
    • YouTube / Google: remove ReelSmith on the Google security settings page.
    • Facebook: Settings & privacy → Settings → Business integrations, then remove ReelSmith.
  • Videos and files stay until you delete them. Deleting a video in ReelSmith deletes its files; uninstalling ReelSmith and deleting its folder removes everything it stored.
  • Data held by the platforms and services (such as videos you posted) is governed by their policies and can be deleted there.
  • Deletion requests. Because ReelSmith stores nothing on its own servers, the steps above delete all data ReelSmith holds. For help, email AyeEyeStoryShort@gmail.com and you’ll get a reply within 30 days.

6. Security

Tokens and keys are kept in your operating system’s secure credential store and are never written to logs or error messages. Connections to the services above use HTTPS. No method of storage or transmission is perfectly secure, so keep your computer and accounts protected.

7. Children

ReelSmith is not intended for anyone under 18 and does not knowingly collect information from children.

8. Your choices and rights

Because your data is on your own computer, you can see, change, export or delete it at any time. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights to access or delete personal information; contact us to use them.

9. Changes and contact

This policy may be updated; the effective date at the top shows the latest version. Questions or requests: AyeEyeStoryShort@gmail.com (Kentucky, United States).
