Narrated short videos, made on your computer.

ReelSmith is a desktop app that turns a topic or story idea into a narrated, captioned video for TikTok, YouTube and Facebook. It writes the script, records the voiceover, makes the pictures and puts the video together. You check each step along the way.

  1. ScriptWritten by AI or by you
  2. NarrationA voice you choose, or your own
  3. ImagesGenerated, uploaded or free stock
  4. VideoCaptions and music added
  5. PostOnly when you press it

What it does

AI or by hand

Let AI write, voice and illustrate a video, or write it yourself, record your own narration and pick your own pictures.

Stories with recurring characters

Story mode plans a complete story up to 10 minutes long and keeps the same characters in every picture.

Ready for each platform

Make vertical or horizontal videos, split long ones into parts, and post to TikTok, YouTube and a Facebook Page.

Your data stays on your computer

ReelSmith runs on your own computer. It has no servers or user accounts of its own, collects no analytics, and never sells your data.

When you connect TikTok, YouTube or Facebook, you sign in on that platform itself; ReelSmith never sees your password. It uploads a video only after you review its caption and settings and press Post.
